Memory

The game keeps your inventory in real-number variables. 22.44 means "you have it". Why 22.44? Only 2009 knows.

How to play: follow the prompts. When in doubt, press ENTER.

Garbage Man was written by Peter C. Davidson in 2009. The original GARBAGE.8xp still runs on a real TI-83/84 Plus or under MirageOS.
